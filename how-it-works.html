QUICK ANSWERS

Questions About Getting Started?

Here are some common questions about our dispatch process.

How do I get started with HAULNEX?

Start by submitting the carrier application. Our team can review your information and discuss your equipment, operation and dispatch requirements.

What information should I provide?

Basic business and contact details, equipment information, preferred operating areas and relevant carrier documentation may be requested.

Do I have to accept every load?

No. The carrier reviews the load details and makes the final decision about accepting or declining a freight opportunity.

Does HAULNEX support different truck types?

We aim to support different equipment categories. Available dispatch support depends on the equipment, freight requirements and operational arrangements.

How is dispatch communication handled?

Communication arrangements are discussed during onboarding so that relevant load information, updates and coordination details can be shared.

Are dispatch fees and agreements discussed beforehand?

Yes. Applicable service terms, fees, responsibilities and agreements should be reviewed and agreed upon before dispatch services begin.
