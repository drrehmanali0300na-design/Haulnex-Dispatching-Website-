WHO WE ARE

More Than Dispatching. Your Business Partner.

HAULNEX Dispatch Services is focused on supporting independent owner-operators and small trucking fleets across the United States.

We understand that managing a trucking business takes more than keeping a truck moving. It requires coordination, communication, organization and attention to every load.

Our goal is to simplify the dispatching process so carriers can focus on what matters most: running their business and moving freight.
