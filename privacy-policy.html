This Privacy Policy describes the information practices of HAULNEX Dispatch Services ("HAULNEX", "we", "our" or "us"). It applies to information collected through our website, contact forms, carrier applications and communications related to our services.

01

Information We Collect

We may collect different categories of information depending on how you interact with HAULNEX and which services you request.

Personal Information

Information you voluntarily provide may include:

  • Full name and contact person's name.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Business or company name.
  • Business location and general contact details.
  • Information submitted through contact forms.
  • Information included in messages and inquiries.

Carrier and Business Information

If you submit a carrier application or inquire about dispatch services, we may collect business-related details such as:

  • USDOT and MC numbers, where provided.
  • Carrier authority status.
  • Truck and trailer types.
  • Number of trucks in your operation.
  • Equipment availability.
  • Preferred routes and operating areas.
  • Dispatch service preferences.
  • Additional business information you choose to provide.

Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected by our hosting provider or website tools, such as:

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Pages visited and general usage data.
  • Approximate access time and referral information.
02

How We Collect Information

We may collect information through the following methods:

  • Direct submissions: When you complete a contact form, carrier application or other website form.
  • Direct communications: When you contact us by email, telephone or another communication channel.
  • Website technology: Through server logs, cookies or similar technologies, where enabled.
  • Service interactions: When you communicate with us about dispatch support or business inquiries.
03

How We Use Information

Information collected may be used for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to inquiries and contact requests.
  • Reviewing carrier applications.
  • Understanding your equipment and dispatch requirements.
  • Communicating about requested services.
  • Providing, maintaining and improving our website.
  • Managing business relationships and service requests.
  • Protecting our website against misuse and unauthorized activity.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not claim to use submitted information for purposes unrelated to the reason it was collected unless permitted by applicable law or with appropriate notice and consent.

04

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only where reasonably necessary for legitimate business operations or as permitted by law.

Service Providers

We may use trusted providers for website hosting, communication, security, storage and other operational services. These providers may process information on our behalf under applicable contractual safeguards.

Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed when required by law, valid legal process or an authorized government request, or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights and safety.

Business Changes

In connection with a merger, restructuring, sale or transfer of business assets, information may be transferred subject to appropriate protections and applicable law.

05

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no website, online transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot promise absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

06

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to inquiries, maintaining business records, providing services, resolving disputes and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable legal requirements. Information that is no longer required may be securely deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

07

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or its service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, understand usage and improve performance.

Types of Technologies

  • Essential technologies: Help support basic website operation and security.
  • Analytics technologies: If enabled, help us understand general website usage.
  • Preference technologies: If enabled, may remember certain website preferences.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website functionality may not work properly if essential cookies are disabled.

The use of analytics or advertising technologies will depend on the tools actually implemented on our website.

08

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

These rights may include:

  • Requesting access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of information, where legally permitted.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing activities.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Requesting a copy of certain information in a portable format.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us through our Contact page. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

We will handle eligible requests in accordance with applicable law.

09

Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website may contain links to external websites, platforms or services operated by third parties.

We do not control the privacy practices, content or security of those third-party services. Visiting an external website is subject to that provider's own terms and privacy policy.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of external services before sharing information with them.

10

Children's Privacy

Our website and dispatch-related services are intended for business users and are not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

11

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, website features or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the revised policy will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

12

Contact Information

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact HAULNEX Dispatch Services through our website.
